# Worldwalker RPG v3.27.2

## Dedicated Naruto Jinchūriki system

- Jinchūriki now have their own expandable Naruto system card instead of being represented as an ordinary skill, class, Kekkei Genkai, or Dōjutsu.
- Every host records the tailed beast as an independent character, with its canon nature transformations and traits, complete potential, currently accessible powers, seal, bond, relationship, control, transformation stage, mastery route, and drawbacks.
- Unmastered hosts receive the large chakra reserve and real risks appropriate to the setting: unwilling-beast resistance, involuntary leakage, bodily damage, berserk escalation, friendly-fire danger, weakened seals, social isolation, political exploitation, hunters, and normally fatal extraction.
- Perfect hosts gain deliberate access to full transformation and the Tailed Beast Ball and no longer suffer unmastered loss-of-control or corrosive-cloak drawbacks. Chakra cost, collateral damage, suppression, seal disruption, extraction risk, and the beast's independent will still matter.
- Creation recognizes explicit Jinchūriki backgrounds, selects the stated beast or a stable lore-valid beast when unspecified, scales starting chakra reserves, and avoids generating an unrelated generic ability from the same host description.
- Naruto's birth and Academy-graduation canon starts now receive structured Kurama profiles at the correct stages: seal pending at birth and unmastered at graduation.
- Older Naruto saves with only the legacy `Jinchuriki` text field migrate into the complete profile without rewriting their existing health or chakra values.
- Character preview shows the host's current access, full canon potential, and dangers before the campaign begins.

## Chakra affinity now affects jutsu learning

- Every Naruto character receives a persistent primary chakra nature, plus any established secondary or learned natures.
- Character preview and the Progress Journal show the affinity, discovery status, learned natures, per-element learning pace, and external nature access.
- Matching elemental jutsu are learned and refined faster and stabilized more efficiently. Off-affinity basic natures remain possible, but normally demand substantially more time, control, instruction, and practice.
- Combined nature transformations still require their component natures plus a Kekkei Genkai, equivalent special mechanism, or a valid original research route.
- Tailed-beast nature transformations are recorded as external access and do not overwrite the host's own natural affinity.

## Player-chosen speed turns

- A decisive player speed advantage no longer repeats the selected attack automatically.
- Combat pauses before the opponent's response, highlights a **Quickened Turn**, and returns every combat control so the player can attack differently, defend, move, heal, control, overwhelm, or flee.
- The enemy responds only after that bonus choice is resolved; status damage and durations tick once for the complete exchange rather than twice.
