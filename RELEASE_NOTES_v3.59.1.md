# v3.59.1 — Campaign-aware political maps

- Naruto shades countries, with hidden-village authority listed separately. Taking Konoha no longer annexes the entire Land of Fire.
- Starting-era history is distinct from current campaign outcomes. Early Rimuru starts do not show an already-founded Tempest; early Naruto starts do not show an established Sound village. Later turns cannot force these outcomes just by passing a date.
- One Piece distinguishes actual control, kingdom sovereignty and pirate protection. Starting-era presets cover Roger-era, East Blue, Summit War and reunion contexts; explicit campaign changes override them.
- Authored regional boundaries replace nearest-landmark wedges in Naruto, Overgeared and Slime. Belto is shown before Valhalla in Satisfy's available early starts. HxH offices and estates no longer claim enormous neighboring regions.
- Bleach realm boards are regional diagrams rather than invented ocean islands, with Seireitei/Las Noches enclosures. The Living World has an extrapolated local river corridor.
- Explicit simple ruler/estate backgrounds initialize holdings locally. More complex descriptions are handled by the opening GM, which must record map ownership. Existing saves are not reinterpreted from old backgrounds.
- Country labels take priority at world zoom; landmark detail takes priority when zoomed in. Map details explain local authority and reference assumptions.

Geography combines established canon, reference research and contextual game extrapolation. It is not a survey-accurate reproduction. No new timeline events/dates were added. No playable destinations were added; Belto is the era display name for the existing Valhalla site. Solo retains its current-floor-only diagram; detailed floor terrain is still a later authoring task.

Compatibility: explicit saved location ownership and political regions remain authoritative. Old saves with a calendar anchor use their original starting era. Saves with no recoverable start use the default era, not a guess based on the current date. Old unrecorded conquests still require a GM correction; the map cannot safely reconstruct them from an old background alone.
