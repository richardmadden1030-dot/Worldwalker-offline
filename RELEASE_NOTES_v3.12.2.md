# Worldwalker RPG v3.12.2

- Skills now use a shared taxonomy covering offense, defense, healing, support, control, mobility, detection, stealth, summons, transformations, crafting, knowledge, social abilities and utility.
- Barriers absorb damage; control can stop retaliation; damage-over-time persists; cleansing removes harmful effects; summons provide temporary support; buffs, movement, detection, stealth and transformations provide distinct tactical benefits.
- Legacy skills are classified locally without an AI call, and noncombat profession or knowledge skills stay out of combat controls.
- Skill cards show clean category, target and duration information. Combat displays active barriers, buffs, summons, debuffs and status durations.
- Shikai and Bankai use transformation mechanics rather than falling through to generic damage.
- Godot ambient embeds were removed in favor of faster native scene, portrait and map effects with world-specific styling and reduced runtime weight.
- Scene transitions now respond to travel, combat and major events, with improved weather, environmental and map-state presentation.
- Scene-art matching and portrait cache behavior were tightened, including a new clinic/hospital scene.
- Desktop and 390-pixel mobile layouts were browser-tested for the updated Skills and Combat interfaces.
