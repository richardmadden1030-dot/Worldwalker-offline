# Worldwalker RPG v3.11.0

## Solo Max-Level Newbie

- Every active floor has a persistent scenario, clear condition, deadline,
  environmental rule, administrator, encounter roster, boss, routes and
  discoverable hidden conditions based on the existing 50-floor structure.
- Ability-copy attempts now preserve their source, condition, progress,
  restriction and slot cost instead of becoming unexplained rewards.
- Foreknowledge is separated into remembered, confirmed, changed and spent
  information so game knowledge can help without silently completing secrets.
- Rivals advance locally between turns. Party roles, artifacts, achievement
  chains and completed-floor reports remain available in the Journal.

## Overgeared

- Combat level, class development and each production discipline now advance
  on separate records.
- Generated classes retain a class questline, stage progress, next unlock and
  unlocked features.
- NPC affinity, guild membership, territory, crafting commissions, personal
  economy and public rankings are persistent and readable in the Journal.

## Crafting and inventory

- Crafting remains a narrative Chronicle activity in every world; there is no
  separate recipe or ingredient-management minigame.
- Ore, ingots, reagents, scraps and similar routine components are kept out of
  the Bag. Named equipment, reusable tools, artifacts, quest objects and other
  memorable finished products persist with grade, effects, limits and source.
- The Skills Journal and Bag use expanded readable cards on desktop and stack
  into a single column on mobile.

No map landmark or timeline date was added or changed in this release.
