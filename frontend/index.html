⚔ Power Summary
How Worldwalker Plays
✒ Type actions, then ADVANCE

Typing an action just adds it to your Action Chat queue — nothing happens yet. Add as many as you like, in order, then press ADVANCE to have the GM resolve them all at once.

⏳ Moment vs. longer skips

Moment (the default) resolves exactly one contextual story beat, never more than 24 hours. Switch the Time Control dropdown to days/weeks/months — or "next major event" — when you want to fast-forward training, travel, or downtime instead of playing it out beat by beat.

🧭 The Advisor is free

Stuck, or just want a read on the situation? ASK THE ADVISOR anytime for a power comparison, world briefing, or strategic suggestion. It never changes your campaign state and never costs a turn — ask it as often as you like.

        How To Play
        
    
        
      
        Add any number of actions to the queue. Queuing is planning only: the GM and world remain silent, and the clock does not move.
        
      
        Choose an amount of time and press ADVANCE to review the plan. The game resolves the queued actions in order, raising difficulty or deferring later actions when the allotted time is insufficient.
        
      
        Each major action, reaction, consequence, and due canon event appears as its own detailed story update. Canon is a living pressure rather than a railroad, so your choices can naturally change it.
        
      
        Hard actions use contextual d100 checks. Your roll plus relevant stats, skills, titles, and circumstances must beat the needed number. High/extreme lethal choices warn you before commitment, and death can be rewound to the pre-Advance checkpoint.
        
      
        AI ROLES
        Main model: Narrator + Rules Referee
        Background model: World/NPC Simulator + Memory Manager
        
      
        LOCAL AI
        LM Studio can run both text roles on your own PC with no per-turn API fees. Open OPTIONS → AI & Portrait Setup, start the LM Studio server, Detect Models, then Save. Portraits require a separately compatible image model.
