# Worldwalker RPG v3.54.0 — Character Life & Legacy

- Companions retain personal goals and independent development.
- Relationships gain locally calculated, reason-backed phases.
- Standing care, training and mentorship orders can produce earned milestones over time.
- Marriage, children, reconciliation, retirement and succession require established evidence and player choice.
- Campaign-shaping deeds become persistent, world-specific legacy records.
- NPC-to-NPC bonds, old companions, contacts and standing instructions migrate into the shared system.
- AI narration and offline choices use the same compact Life context with no additional AI request.
- Includes the v3.53.1 Chronicle focus repair: completed turns open at their first newly generated update.
