# Worldwalker RPG v3.41.0

## A world that keeps moving

- Long skips now distribute major action, companion, communication, progression, and world beats across the dates they occurred.
- Delegated companions and recurring NPCs continue established work independently. NPC growth follows their own goals and does not scale automatically to the player.
- Skills retain a readable history of new applications, mastery, breakthroughs, and evolution.
- Each world receives setting-specific downtime context, while grounded incoming messages can react to important developments without another AI call.
- A local prompt-budget manager selects the most relevant save facts for each AI role, reducing repeated context overhead.

## Satisfy class reception

- Overgeared characters normally enter Satisfy as Beginners and receive a class through a narrative class-change opportunity, quest, NPC, item, achievement, unusual behavior, or hidden condition.
- Starting Archetype now guides the opportunities the player seeks rather than awarding a class automatically.
- Character creation still offers explicit Hidden and Legendary class starts. Those classes remain original, complete, and unique.

## Recovery and readability

- Diagnostics now offers targeted recovery for scene, combat, progression, abilities, NPC/party, and world-state records.
- Skill details display developed applications and evolution history without exposing raw internal data.
- Player-facing patch notes appear once for v3.41.0.
