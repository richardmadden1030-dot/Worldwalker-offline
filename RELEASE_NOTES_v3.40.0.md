# Worldwalker RPG v3.40.0

## A more consistent Game Master

- The GM now carries a compact live scene ledger containing the exact location, present characters, immediate danger, unresolved question, and current objective. This makes scene-to-scene replies less likely to contradict what just happened.
- Meaningful mechanical rewards are checked against the narrated outcome. Mastery, transformations, major growth, titles, and lasting conditions can no longer exist only in prose without being flagged or recorded.
- Canon divergence now tracks which later events were affected, while promises, debts, long-running duties, and delayed consequences can mature naturally in later turns.
- Pacing reacts to repeated combat, training, social, exploration, political, and recovery beats so the story can vary its next development without forcing unrelated events.
- The GM learns presentation preferences only from turns the player explicitly rates positively. It never uses those preferences to change facts or difficulty.

## Clearer answers at lower cost

- Advisor answers now include an expandable evidence trail showing the live mechanics, campaign memory, and save state behind the answer.
- Lore retrieval is selective: routine actions avoid unnecessary lore payloads, while canon-heavy questions, openings, major events, and named-setting questions still receive the relevant sources.
- If an AI response omits a required choice, moves the player without explanation, or narrates a lasting breakthrough without mechanics, the game makes one focused repair attempt instead of rerunning the entire turn.

## Update notes

- Each update now presents its player-facing patch notes once on first launch for that browser and account. After the player dismisses them, they stay out of the way until the version changes again.

## Testing

- Added focused regression coverage for scene continuity, obligations, delayed consequences, outcome synchronization, canon impact tracking, pacing, learned presentation preferences, selective lore retrieval, response repair, Advisor evidence, and one-time patch notes.
- All 742 automated tests pass, and the update window was verified in the real browser with no console errors.
