# Worldwalker RPG v3.44.1 — Reliable Economy and Poneglyph Chronicle

- Preserves fractional prices and adds exact Gold/Silver/Copper arithmetic for the Slime world.
- Records purchases, rewards, recurring income, expenses, and payments in a compact money ledger.
- Converts unpaid recurring expenses into payable obligations instead of negative currency.
- Supports deterministic purchases with secondary currencies such as tokens or faction points.
- Preserves reusable item effects, categories, ratings, and restrictions after purchase.
- Prevents narrative-only worlds such as Bleach and JJK from acquiring accidental money bookkeeping.
- Keeps starting wealth separate from combat power language.
- Applies the approved bright, cel-shaded Poneglyph stone presentation to One Piece Chronicle turns.
