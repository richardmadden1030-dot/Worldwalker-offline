# Worldwalker RPG v3.16.1

## Naruto audio cues

- Plays a short Naruto cue whenever the player confirms Advance.
- Plays a hand-sign cue when beginning a Naruto original-character campaign.
- Plays the Pain cue when beginning the Pain canon-character start.
- Plays the same Pain cue when the canon timeline reaches **Pain's Assault on Konoha**.
- Plays the Naruto death cue when a Naruto player character dies, including deaths reached through time skips and combat.
- Custom cues respect the sound-effects setting, do not stack over one another, and temporarily lower active background music.

## Packaging

- All four supplied MP3 files were re-encoded without metadata at compact browser-compatible bitrates.
- Combined packaged size is approximately 361 KB, so the desktop and phone-host downloads remain lightweight.
