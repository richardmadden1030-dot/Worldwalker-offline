# Worldwalker RPG v3.48.0 — Tactical Battles: Naruto & One Piece

- Move horizontally or vertically, aim learned abilities, and preview their exact affected tiles.
- Naruto techniques retain individual mechanics, summons, status effects and transformation rules.
- One Piece supports Devil Fruit applications, Haki, Zoan forms, Logia counters, Sea-Prism Stone, Geppo and Room: Shambles.
- Movement and extra activations use combat stats; enemies retain narrative or canon-relative strength instead of scaling to the player.
- Combat is server-authoritative, retry-safe, multiplayer-aware and available on desktop and phone.
- Existing Haki saves migrate automatically, while outcomes return to the Chronicle without invented rewards or bounties.
