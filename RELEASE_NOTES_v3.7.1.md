# Worldwalker RPG 3.7.1

Version 3.7.1 rebalances player agency and progression without weakening
Nightmare difficulty.

- Story, Adventurer, and Veteran now explicitly support power-fantasy play.
- Logically possible player actions resolve decisively instead of being
  downgraded into vague attempts or arbitrary failures.
- Diplomacy, politics, and strategy are challenged through NPC and faction
  responses: conditions, counteroffers, obligations, suspicion, retaliation,
  competing agendas, and betrayal.
- Canon characters retain their established personalities and motives; the
  player gains meaningful leverage without mind-controlling them.
- A named intended effect plus a plausible world-valid method bypasses an
  unnecessary difficulty check below Nightmare.
- Only literal physical, metaphysical, prerequisite, or established-state
  contradictions are treated as impossible.
- Sustained training gains are larger on the three lower difficulties and
  broad combat regimens improve supporting combat attributes as well as the
  primary stat.
- Six months of rigorous broad Naruto training can establish a jōnin-level
  combat benchmark without falsely granting an official village rank.
- Plausible named power-training routes become assured progression milestones
  after a difficulty-appropriate commitment instead of repeated luck gates.
- Nightmare retains its previous assessment, check, and training behavior.

The save schema remains 13 and v3.7.0 campaigns remain compatible.
