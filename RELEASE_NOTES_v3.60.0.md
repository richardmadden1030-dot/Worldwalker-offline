# v3.60.0 — Living World: Persistent Plans

- Important established NPCs and factions can receive persistent, staged plans from the existing GM turn, grounded in recorded motives, resources and opportunities. No extra background AI calls are introduced.
- Routine, unopposed work advances locally against actual elapsed time. Prerequisite plans must finish first. New plans cannot retroactively consume the entire turn in which they were created; retries cannot count time twice.
- Contested stages stop for narrative resolution. Conquests, negotiations, new powers and major personal choices are not automatic timer rewards. The GM records resulting territory and NPC location changes through the existing map/knowledge-aware state paths.
- Plans can link to existing standing intents. Cancelled or paused instructions do not keep progressing associated work, and dead/incapacitated/imprisoned/missing actors cannot continue routine work locally.
- Plan milestones are short and capped at two local reports per turn. Hidden plans require an explicit disclosure source/channel before reporting. Pending reports survive the cap; the full private ledger is not exposed through public state.
- Successful outcomes can establish durable narrative benefits or a specific safer-route travel improvement. Route modifiers do not stack and require an explicit causal revocation to end.
- No new management dashboard, mandatory chores, timeline dates or map destinations.
- Chronicle writing guidance favors short complete sentences and clear subjects. Distinct supplementary facts stay in separate paragraphs; exact repeats and empty placeholders are omitted without truncating the narrative.
- One Piece uses the approved flat blue-gray poneglyph with locally bundled angular engraved lettering. Special events, discoveries, rewards and marked milestones use red stone. All text appears together, without a typing delay. Existing records use explicit event tags/headings for their color.

Scope: this extends existing standing-order, faction, NPC, knowledge and map systems. The GM still authors and resolves meaningful plans; local simulation schedules and preserves them. It does not prewrite every faction’s future or guarantee that every character is assigned a plan. Map movement and ownership changes continue to use established narrative state updates rather than a second autonomous conquest simulator.

Other worlds retain their existing visual designs.
