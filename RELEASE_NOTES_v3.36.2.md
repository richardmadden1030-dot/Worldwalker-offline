# Worldwalker RPG v3.36.2

- Original powers now use a persistent, mechanic-first uniqueness check across JJK techniques, Nen, Devil Fruits, Zanpakutō, hidden classes, Naruto bloodlines/special powers, and the equivalent systems in every other world. A reroll changes what the power actually does instead of attaching a new subtitle to a repeated template.
- JJK's small finished-technique pool has been replaced by combinable themes, operations, activation conditions, applications, limitations, and growth rules. Player-authored techniques remain locked to the name and central mechanism stated in the background.
- Generic Devil Fruits now vary by fruit family and governing mechanic while retaining canonical seawater, Sea-Prism Stone, Haki, and awakening rules. Canon characters keep canon abilities.
- Naruto, Bleach, and JJK receive compact world-themed desktop cursors: kunai, Zanpakutō, and Sukuna finger. Naruto uses a spinning shuriken as its busy/loading indicator. Touch devices keep their native controls and reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- Release verification confirms all seven Bleach tracks, all four Naruto tracks, and the optimized Naruto event cues remain present in both Windows and phone packages.
