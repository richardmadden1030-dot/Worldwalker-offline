WORLDWALKER MUSIC

Drop MP3 or MP4 music files into the folder for the world where they should play.
Tracks in Shared can play in every world. M4A, OGG and WAV audio are also scanned.

The game rescans when a campaign/world loads or when you press the refresh button.
MP4 playback depends on the audio codec installed on the computer; MP3 is the most portable choice.

Bleach and Naruto include bundled default soundtracks. Those tracks ship in every release and
can be supplemented with additional files in the corresponding world folder.
