# Worldwalker RPG v3.52.0 — The Living World Director

- Repeated choices now create grounded follow-up opportunities based on what the player actually keeps doing.
- Known NPCs can initiate contact about remembered goals and promises.
- Vague typed actions show the game's interpretation before they are queued.
- Clean successes, mixed outcomes, setbacks and reactions are distinguished instead of forcing a downside into every result.
- Choose an Action adds shopping, meaningful finances/property, celebrations, mentoring, recruiting and territory management.
- Generated-event history prevents accidental duplicates and migrates into old saves automatically.
- Interpretation, eligibility, history and duplicate checks run locally without another AI request.

Example: after several meaningful training choices, the player may receive a specialist lesson or practical test because their sustained practice has become noticeable. A random unrelated crisis is not inserted merely to make the turn eventful.
