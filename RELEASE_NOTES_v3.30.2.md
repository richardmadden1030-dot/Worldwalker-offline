# Worldwalker RPG 3.30.2

This hotfix synchronizes established Jinchuriki story events with character
mechanics and restores detailed Advisor power comparisons.

- A tailed-beast transfer confirmed in campaign canon now creates the missing
  structured Jinchuriki profile even if an older AI response only narrated it.
- Recovery requires explicit evidence that the player received the beast. A
  normal Nine-Tails event or mention of another host cannot trigger it.
- Later bond, cloak, chakra-sharing, and partnership events determine the
  recovered mastery and control stage.
- Missing host stat and chakra-reserve bonuses are applied exactly once and
  recorded with their source and acquisition turn.
- Existing campaigns are repaired when loaded. Yahiko's recorded turn-64
  Kurama transfer resolves as Kurama, Cooperative, 72% control.
- Questions comparing the player with an opponent, organization, or group no
  longer get intercepted by the local self-only power summary.
- Comparison questions use the full Advisor model with current campaign stats,
  rosters, canon estimates, divergences, and history.
- Power comparisons now explicitly request a current bar chart, even when the
  player says "compare" rather than asking for a graph by name.

Save schema remains 19.
