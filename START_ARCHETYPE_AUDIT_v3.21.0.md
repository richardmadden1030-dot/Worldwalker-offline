# Starting archetype audit — v3.21.0

No archetype was deleted in this release. Every current choice creates a valid
mechanical package, and silently deleting one would remove an existing play
fantasy. The following labels should be considered for a later creation-screen
cleanup after player feedback:

- **One Piece — Roguish Fighter:** rename to **Trick Fighter**. “Roguish” is
  vague, while the intended identity is deception, improvised weapons and
  dirty-fighting tactics. Do not merge Navigator, Shipwright, Medic or
  Archaeologist merely because they are noncombat roles; crew jobs are central
  to this setting.
- **Hunter x Hunter — Tracker / Beast Hunter:** keep both but sharpen the copy.
  Tracker should emphasize pursuit, wilderness signs and locating people;
  Beast Hunter should emphasize magical-beast ecology, capture and field care.
  Information Broker is intentionally a career fantasy rather than a fighting
  style and should remain.
- **Naruto — Ninjutsu Student / Genjutsu Student:** rename to **Ninjutsu
  Specialist** and **Genjutsu Specialist** for consistency with the other
  choices. Samurai should remain because it changes embodied fighting style and
  access even when the origin is not the Iron Country start.
- **Solo Max-Level Newbie — All-Rounder / Melee / Ranged:** these are the least
  flavorful labels in the game. Prefer **Adaptive Climber**, **Vanguard**, and
  **Marksman** while preserving their current mechanics. They should not be
  deleted until preview text makes the replacements equally understandable.
- **Overgeared:** keep all 21. They are numerous, but v3.20 made every one a
  distinct playable class family with skills, features, quest and advancement.
  The better future change is category filters, not deletion.
- **Bleach — Kido Caster / Kaido Healer:** keep both and consistently render
  them as **Kidō Caster** and **Kaidō Healer** so players can distinguish the
  spell discipline from healing. Tactical Officer should remain as the command
  and analysis route not covered by the four academy combat arts.
- **Reincarnated as a Slime — Brawler Monster / Beast-kin Warrior:** these
  overlap most. A later revision should replace Brawler Monster with
  **Adaptive Monster**, focused on intrinsic biology and evolution, while Beast-
  kin Warrior remains the trained weapon/body-combat route.
- **Custom World:** keep the broad labels. The custom setting description is
  what gives them meaning, and narrower built-in assumptions would reduce
  flexibility.

Recommended order: rename the confusing labels first, add creation-screen role
filters second, and only consider removing an option after telemetry or player
feedback shows that its preview and starting package remain indistinguishable
from another choice.
