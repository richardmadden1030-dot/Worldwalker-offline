# Worldwalker RPG v3.29.0

## Persistent narrative intentions

- Ongoing commands such as caring for someone, training children, maintaining security, enforcing a policy, or continuing a project now remain active without being repeated every turn.
- Delegated responsibilities continue in the background without consuming the player's entire action window.
- Personal routines share the player's available time with new actions instead of replacing them.
- Routine maintenance stays out of the Chronicle; meaningful milestones, completion, obstacles, and interruptions are surfaced naturally.
- Persistent intentions accumulate elapsed time, pause when temporarily blocked, resume when circumstances permit, and end when completed, cancelled, superseded, failed, or made impossible.
- Empty Advances still preserve the exact prior itinerary, while durable commands are separated from that itinerary so they are not mechanically repeated as one-off actions forever.

## Naruto chakra affinities

- Natural chakra affinity, learned elemental proficiency, and mastered nature transformation are now separate mechanical fields.
- Ordinary shinobi normally begin with one natural affinity. Learning another basic nature creates a proficiency, not a second innate affinity.
- Multiple innate elemental affinities require a recorded Kekkei Genkai, Kekkei Tōta, or another explicit canon-valid mechanism.
- Naruto's canon starts use latent Wind affinity; Yahiko uses trained Water affinity; Pain records all five mastered natures as Rinnegan-enabled proficiency while leaving Nagato's unconfirmed natural affinity uninvented.
- Tailed-beast nature transformations remain external access and do not overwrite the host's natural affinity.
- The campaign preview and character panel now clearly label natural affinities, learned proficiencies, mastered natures, and special mastery sources.
- The GM receives the same authoritative affinity distinctions when deciding what jutsu can be learned and how quickly.
