# Worldwalker RPG v3.38.0

## World-depth simulation

- One Piece records why a bounty changed, separates demonstrated strength from notoriety and Government threat, builds multi-route original-island dossiers, awards Haki through real breakthroughs, and generates proportional newspapers, rumors, Marine orders and rival attention.
- Hunter x Hunter separately tracks Ten, Zetsu, Ren, Gyo, En, Shu, Ko, Ken, Ryu and Hatsu; records opponent knowledge and enforceable Nen vows; preserves a Hatsu's governing rule as it gains applications; and supports specialty-driven noncombat Hunter work.
- Naruto adds clearance-aware village intelligence, promotion records based on service/leadership/recommendations/politics, nine broad shinobi specialties, and a tailed-beast relationship that remembers respect, coercion, promises and shared danger.
- Solo Max-Level Newbie floors now have canon-status labels, scenario rules, factions, ecology, hidden/alternate clears, rewards and distinct administrators. Foreknowledge, copy conditions, System notices and build synergy receive structured records.
- Overgeared classes evolve from behavior and accomplishments across combat, magic, command, religion, politics, exploration, commerce, social play, production and monster taming. NPC affinity reasons, important equipment history, significant economy and a moving ranking ecosystem are retained.
- Reincarnated as a Slime adds synthesis analysis, naming consequences, nation records, autonomous named subordinates, evolution routes and progressive Great Sage-style analysis.
- Bleach adds squad-placement records, division culture, a living Zanpakutō relationship, a learned Kidō reference, spiritual-duty history and concrete Reiatsu interactions.
- Jujutsu Kaisen adds a mechanical revealing-one's-hand bonus, enforced vow breach state, mission dossiers, grade/reliability/political separation, and domain clashes based on refinement, barrier, output, range and compatibility.

## Prompt and cost efficiency

- The GM receives only world-depth instructions relevant to the current task and action. Frequent message/background calls receive a compact reminder; creation/audit calls retain the complete contract.
- New ledgers, deduplication, progression bookkeeping, floor assembly, domain comparison and System notices run locally without extra AI calls.

## Presentation and fixes

- New expandable world-progression cards expose the added records without cluttering the collapsed Skills view.
- Long major/canon-event titles now remain in their own flow area instead of overlapping the event description. Desktop and phone layouts were tested with an intentionally oversized title.
- Existing saves are migrated locally with empty/safe defaults for the new records.
