# Worldwalker RPG v3.14.0

## Narrative political map

- Land-owning canon factions now appear as bounded shaded regions with clear borders.
- Adjacent territory under the same controller joins visually into one possession.
- Conquest, annexation, independence, overthrow and territorial collapse can change the atlas permanently.
- Player-founded territory can become a full polity with its own faction and world clock.
- Claimed land is sized by its narrative scale, from a holding or village through a country or realm.
- A ruler receives a brief Chronicle update about public feeling every few months or after a rapid change.
- Rebellions, unrest and political crises remain ordinary ongoing story events rather than exposed management statistics.
- Hidden polity bookkeeping is excluded from public campaign state and no capital, stability, conflict list or authority score was added to the UI.

## Reliability

- Canon ownership now participates in background territorial conflicts even before a location receives a custom story override.
- Explicitly unclaimed canon land no longer snaps back to its original controller.
- New land-owning powers automatically join the same faction and clock systems used by established powers.
