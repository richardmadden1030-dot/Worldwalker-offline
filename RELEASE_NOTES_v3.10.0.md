# Worldwalker RPG v3.10.0

## World-system parity

- One Piece now records Devil Fruit identity, type, abilities, limits,
  counters and awakening state alongside separate Observation, Armament and
  Conqueror's Haki development.
- Hunter × Hunter now keeps Nen secret until learned and records category,
  Ten, Zetsu, Ren, Hatsu rules, vows, costs, counters and growth.
- Naruto now separates official rank from combat power and keeps village,
  clan, nature affinities, bloodline, jutsu, summons and transformations in a
  single synchronized shinobi record.
- Solo Max-Level Newbie now has a System record for floor, stat points, copied
  ability slots, achievements and hidden conditions.
- Overgeared now has a Satisfy record for class rarity, secondary class,
  crafting mastery, recipes, specialties, guild and NPC affinity.
- Reincarnated as a Slime now records species, evolution stage, naming,
  magicule capacity, resistances and the Intrinsic/Extra/Unique/Ultimate Skill
  hierarchy.

## Starts, timeline safety, and presentation

- Every ordinary built-in origin receives deterministic mechanical identity,
  equipment, a world-system state, and a readable opening quest before AI is
  called.
- Every canon-character preset uses a complete deterministic stat sheet and a
  normalized background, quest, faction and reputation package. Relevant
  starts now begin inside their live story situation.
- Existing future canon events are hidden by character-knowledge mode and now
  carry causal dependencies. No event was added and no date was changed.
- Hunter × Hunter, Solo, Overgeared and Slime have additional campaign-start
  choices drawn exclusively from landmarks already present on their maps.
- The Skills Journal presents dedicated world-themed progression cards and
  keeps their structured values synchronized with the legacy simulation fields.
- Party and character labels use rank, role, class, license or species instead
  of displaying a false level in settings without canonical levels.

No map landmark or timeline date was added in this release.
