# Worldwalker RPG 3.8.1

This patch adds a new canon-character start and greatly expands One Piece
campaign creation.

- Play as Pain immediately after Yahiko's death, while Nagato and Konan are
  first transforming Akatsuki and the Six Paths system is still incomplete.
- Pain begins with a mechanically represented Rinnegan, nascent Deva Path,
  Outer Path statue, chakra-receiver network, injuries, relationships,
  faction authority, established knowledge, and a branching opening quest.
- The Pain start explicitly permits following canon, preserving Yahiko's
  original ideals, redesigning the Paths, or taking Akatsuki in a new direction.
- One Piece now offers more than thirty starting locations across the East,
  North, South and West Blues, the Grand Line, World Government territory,
  Revolutionary territory, and the New World.
- Every selectable One Piece location has an opening premise, and every new
  location has a corresponding map node.
