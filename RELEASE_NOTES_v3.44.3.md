# Worldwalker RPG v3.44.3

## A More Causal and Efficient Game Master

- Player intent, targets, methods, duration, standing orders, and lethality are parsed locally before narration.
- Ordinary success no longer receives manufactured drama; complications require established causes and are limited to one on an ordinary beat.
- NPC reactions require a believable information source, and malformed information records repair locally.
- Moment, event, and time-skip prompts receive explicit temporal stop conditions.
- Sustained training receives a deterministic local progression plan before prose is generated.
- Canon identity and world-role estimates give the Advisor a direct fallback for named characters without numeric sheets.
- Player-approved examples are chosen for relevance instead of randomly.
- Suggested actions are deduplicated, diversified, and checked against known contacts, location, and finished combat.
- Repeated filler, duplicate sentences, generic danger notices, and stock manufactured-negative lines are removed locally.
- Narratively gained Sharingan and other Dōjutsu repair into Naruto's dedicated panel, including old saves where the power only existed in Chronicle history or the skill list.
- After a turn, the Chronicle opens at the first new update instead of scrolling past it to the bottom.
- Naruto scroll roll results now use dark ink and solid success/failure fills instead of low-contrast mint text on parchment.
- Additional malformed-state guards prevent string-shaped campaign fields from crashing GM context assembly.

Automated coverage: 814 tests, plus frontend syntax validation and packaged-executable smoke testing.
