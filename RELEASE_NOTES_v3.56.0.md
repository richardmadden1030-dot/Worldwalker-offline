# Worldwalker RPG 3.56.0 — Living Atlas

- The desktop atlas now fills the viewport around its compact controls and selected-place inspector.
- Mobile has a dedicated bottom Map tab with touch pan, zoom, and a readable place sheet.
- Every bundled world map is at least 4096 pixels wide for cleaner zooming.
- Naruto receives a new label-free high-detail atlas so live markers and changing political ownership remain authoritative.
- Naruto's five great countries, Amegakure, and the Land of Iron use stable country-shaped regions rather than overlapping radial blobs.
- Naruto landmark placement was recalibrated and expanded with supported canon locations.
- Locations without a canon-fixed coordinate are not pinned to a fabricated position.
- Player-founded territory can still begin at one hex, grow, merge, split, and repaint through narrative ownership changes.
