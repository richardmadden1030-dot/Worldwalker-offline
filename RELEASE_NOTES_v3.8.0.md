# Worldwalker RPG 3.8.0

This release rebuilds campaign creation around mechanical start packages.

- Canon presets now seed signature abilities, equipment, status, knowledge,
  immediate quests, and scene conditions instead of relying on opening prose.
- High-status original origins now grant the rank, faction, class, license,
  mastery, or evolved state named by the selection.
- Impossible origin/era combinations are corrected when safe or surfaced as a
  clear alternate-history warning in Preview.
- Major maps now include timeline landmarks used by the simulation, and the
  Hunter x Hunter and Tensura timelines extend into later major arcs.
- Secret, hostile, or distant factions remain known but are not directly
  messageable until the player earns or discovers a real channel.
- New archetypes: Archaeologist; Beast Hunter; Blacklist Hunter; Sealing
  Specialist; Sensor; Puppet User; Trap Specialist; Alchemist; Magic Crafter.
- New eras include Marineford Eve, the Straw Hat reunion, Yorknew and Chimera
  Ant periods, Uchiha Massacre/Shippuden/Fourth War periods, early Tower races,
  Satisfy's launch/Reidan, and Tempest's Orc Disaster/nation/Demon Lord periods.

Bleach is intentionally left for the next focused world pass.
