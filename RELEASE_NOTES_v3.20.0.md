# Worldwalker RPG v3.20.0

## Satisfy classes, progression, and safer combat starts

- All 21 Overgeared creation archetypes now begin with two named class skills,
  working class features, a role-specific quest, useful suggested actions, and
  defined specialization/evolution paths.
- Class-aligned actions receive a real mechanical bonus and advance their own
  class track. Healing, support, defense, scouting, command, commerce,
  negotiation, and companion play earn meaningful XP alongside combat.
- Summoner and Beast Master companions are persistent contracts with loyalty,
  levels, shared-action history, and progression notices.
- Overgeared and Solo Max-Level Newbie now show Level and XP prominently in the
  character sheet and their world-system windows.
- XP, levels, skills, titles, quests, class milestones, companion growth, and
  floor progress are presented as native Satisfy or Tower/System notices.
- A searchable class encyclopedia exposes Satisfy's full canon class breadth
  as inspiration without forcing original characters onto Grid's path.
- Overgeared openings, leads, quests, and backstories are instructed to follow
  the selected role; unrelated characters no longer inherit Grid's crafting
  identity or personal story.
- Combat no longer begins from figurative phrases such as “strike a deal,”
  tense conversations, or unsupported AI combat patches. Explicit attacks and
  unavoidable incoming attacks still begin combat immediately.
- AI setup now validates the selected provider, key, and model before claiming
  the connection is ready.

No save-schema change was required.
