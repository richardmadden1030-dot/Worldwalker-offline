# Worldwalker RPG v3.33.1

- Character age now advances whenever the campaign calendar crosses a full year.
- Multi-year skips apply every completed year at once and add one compact birthday Chronicle update.
- Existing saves from before age tracking are repaired once from their current campaign year. A character still showing 17 in Year 4 will load as 20.
- Unknown and descriptive ages remain untouched instead of being guessed.
- Includes the complete local visual-feedback update introduced in v3.33.0.
