# Worldwalker RPG v3.36.1

- Player-authored ability names, governing mechanisms, power language, affiliations, and appearance facts are extracted before generation and reapplied after local generation, AI authorship, and rerolls.
- JJK momentum-storing techniques retain that mechanism; named Bleach Zanpakutō retain their exact player-authored name.
- A named Zanpakutō now synchronizes its release profile, Shikai/Bankai names, weapon entry, portrait identity, and continuity memory.
- Advisor power-comparison questions always receive a direct local answer and readable comparison chart using current player stats, tracked NPC evidence, and world-native canon/role estimates.
- Repeated narrator events can no longer announce a title the player already owns.
- Player-facing power summaries use one world-native balanced tier plus a clearly separate peak-specialty tier; generic labels remain internal diagnostics only.
