# Worldwalker RPG v3.55.0 — Tactical Combat Overhaul

- Naruto, One Piece and Bleach encounters can open a responsive server-authoritative grid battle.
- Movement is orthogonal, free from the attack action, limited by the active combatant's speed, and routed around setting-appropriate obstacles.
- Learned abilities preview and resolve their actual target shape; unsupported powers remain visibly unavailable instead of becoming invented generic attacks.
- Every present combat-support ally is directly controlled by the player and retains their own stats, abilities, portrait, forms and speed bonus activation.
- Status effects, shields, resource costs, cooldowns, transformations, summons, lethal/nonlethal outcomes and battle receipts resolve locally.
- Bleach adds locally classified Kidō, Shunpo, synchronized Zanpakutō releases and release-gated applications.
- Naruto and One Piece retain their authored canon move/power libraries, unique mechanics, transformation art and effect families.
- Enemy strength comes from canon, role and established narrative evidence rather than player-level scaling; reusable enemy attacks avoid combat-generation calls.
- Signed-in phone battles now use the correct session endpoint and bearer fallback, while LAN browsers no longer require `crypto.randomUUID()`.
- Tactical moves make no AI call. One optional GM recap turns the finished mechanical battle into Chronicle prose.

Next major checkpoint: return to the approved Living Map prototype for production integration after combat release verification.
