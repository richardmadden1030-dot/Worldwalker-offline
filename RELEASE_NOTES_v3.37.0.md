# Worldwalker RPG v3.37.0

- Chronicle updates now arrive through world-native presentations: Naruto scrolls, Bleach Hell Butterflies, One Piece Poneglyph records, and game-system popups for Overgeared and Solo Max-Level Newbie.
- Companion pairs can discover persistent combination abilities that the GM remembers and develops through later use.
- Player-founded territory and headquarters can begin at one map hex, expand with narrative control, gain names, and receive upgrades without turning land management into a separate strategy game.
- Downtime can produce world-specific surprises, while messages and recent chats now feed back into the simulation as established conversations and commitments.
- Important keepsakes are proposed as legacy trophies and are saved only after the player explicitly accepts them.
- One Piece receives a Straw Hat Jolly Roger cursor. All new presentation effects support mobile layouts and reduced-motion settings.
