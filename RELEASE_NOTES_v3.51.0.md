# Worldwalker RPG v3.51.0

## Choose or Write Any Action

- Added a compact world-aware action picker for people, training, travel, work,
  organizations, combat, personal needs and setting-specific activities.
- Added rest, sleep, meals, treatment, relaxation, planning and equipment care.
- Relationship portraits now open interaction choices for that specific person.
- Duration choices can turn suitable actions into standing instructions.
- Favorites, recent actions, ongoing orders and the current plan remain visible
  inside the picker.
- The normal freeform composer remains available for anything not listed.
- All menu generation remains local and adds no AI request.
